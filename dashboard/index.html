Drill-down

What you should and shouldn't take from this

  • Personal, not population. Every score compares you to your own pre-flight baseline. We deliberately avoid implying a population "normal" — n=4 is too small to define one.
  • "Within baseline noise" means we can't tell. If a post-flight value sits inside the spread of your three pre-flight swabs, we say so explicitly. That's not "you recovered" — it's "we can't distinguish from your normal variation."
  • Color = "shifted," not "dangerous." The avatar's red regions show how far a body site's microbial community has moved from your own pre-flight baseline — it doesn't distinguish a beneficial shift from a harmful one. To answer "is this a concerning shift?" open the drilldown: any species on a curated literature-flagged opportunist list (e.g., Staphylococcus aureus, Moraxella catarrhalis) carries a ⚠ badge with a citation.
  • No clinical recommendations. This is a research-data viewer, not a medical tool. Anything actionable should go through your flight surgeon.
  • No in-flight CBC. Blood draws happened before and after the mission, not during. The CBC plots intentionally have a gap between L-3 and R+1 — we don't interpolate across it.
